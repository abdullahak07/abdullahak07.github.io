01 · Research

Can an AI model truly forget?

My PhD asks whether information is actually removed—and whether that claim survives privacy attacks, representation analysis and deployment-time transformations.

01

Remove

Selective forgetting methods that reduce targeted influence without full retraining.

02

Measure

Audit forgetting, utility, privacy and representation metrics against retraining.

03

Attack

Stress-test unlearned models after transformations such as low-bit quantisation.

04

Generalise

Extend unlearning into multimodal and vision-language systems with interacting pathways.

Interactive research idea

Forgetting can look successful—until deployment changes the model.

Conceptually simulate the logic behind quantisation-induced recovery.

05 · About

Methods, systems and teaching.

I am a final-stage PhD researcher in Computer Science at Murdoch University and a university educator across two Australian institutions. My work focuses on trustworthy and privacy-preserving AI, especially machine unlearning in neural, multimodal and vision-language systems.

I build reproducible pipelines for unlearning, privacy attacks, robustness testing, quantisation, representation analysis and statistical evaluation.
